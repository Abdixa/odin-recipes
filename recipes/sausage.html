Description

Italian sausage is one of the best recipe you can ever taste
if you ever come to Italy.

It is a light weight you can have whenever you want it. You should
really try it and you will never be able to skip a day without it.

Ingredients

  • Pancetta
  • Amatriciana
  • Orecchiette
  • Tagliattelle

Steps

  1. Place thawed sausage links in a single layer in a large skillet.
  2. Add about a half-inch or enough cold water to come halfway up the sides of the links.
  3. Cover the skillet with a lid and bring the water to a gentle simmer
    over medium-high heat, cooking for 8 to 12 minutes.
  4. Remove the lid and let any remaining water evaporate completely.
  5. Continue cooking the sausages in the dry pan for 5 to 7 minutes, turning them
    frequently until the casings are nicely browned and the internal temperature reaches 160°F.
